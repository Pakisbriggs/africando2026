About the Conference

The Premier U.S.–Africa Trade Platform

AfrICANDO is the premier U.S.–Africa trade and investment Conference/Expo, facilitating business cooperation and engagement with the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA). It provides a unique platform for stakeholders to connect, collaborate, and position African markets as a hub for global trade.

With Africa's regional integration and global markets expanding, AfrICANDO 2026 returns to Africa and is hosted by the Abia State Government, co-organized by the Foundation for Democracy in Africa (FDA) and the Abia Global Chamber of Commerce. Over three days, participants will explore plenary sessions, policy forums, business matchmaking, an expo hall, and the prestigious Medal of Glory Awards (MOGA) Gala Dinner.

Whether you seek market entry, investment prospects, policy updates, or strategic alliances, AfrICANDO 2026 is the starting point for meaningful U.S.–Africa commercial engagement.

International Guest Services

Security & Transportation Package

For international delegates arriving in Abia State, the event organizers have arranged a dedicated security and transportation package. This covers your entire stay — from airport arrival to daily venue transfers — so you can focus entirely on the conference.

Both Port Harcourt International Airport and Akanu Ibiam International Airport (Enugu) are serviced. All transfers are handled by vetted, event-designated logistics providers coordinated directly by the conference team.

  • Airport pickup from Port Harcourt or Enugu Airport (with name-board greeting)
  • Daily hotel-to-venue-to-hotel shuttle (all three conference days)
  • Dedicated security escort throughout the conference period
  • Airport drop-off on departure day
  • 24/7 emergency contact line for international guests
  • Optional: Nov 20 sightseeing excursion shuttle across Abia State

Payment for this package is collected separately from your conference registration and hotel booking. Your payment receipt and transport itinerary will be emailed immediately upon checkout.
